Model equations

Bowling load = (release/143)² × action factor × balls bowled / 100

Batting load = 0.35 × (bat speed/118)² × balls faced / 100

Fielding load = per-throw weight × keeper factor × throws / 100 (0.50 outfield, 0.25 keeper, keeper factor 1.5)

All three are scaled so the sample's base-case maximum equals 100. Fatigue: F(t+1) = F(t)·exp(−spacing/τ) + load per match, with season load split over 14 base matches; extra playoff matches add exposure at the same per-match load. Bands: Low below the squad median, Moderate up to the 80th percentile, High above.
